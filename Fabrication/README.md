# Fabrication 
## Overview
After Optimizing the Antenna Dimensions through HFSS Simulations and the ML- based Optimization workflow,
the final antenna design was Fabricated for Experimental Validation.

---

## Fabrication Process
- Designed the Optimized antenna geometry in ANSYS HFSS.
- Prepared the fabrication layout.
- Fabricated the microstrip Patch Antenna on the Selected Substrate.
- Inspected the fabricated prototype before measurements

---

---

## Materials 
- Substrate: FR4 (dielectric)
- Copper Thickness: 0.035mm
- Feed type: Inset Feed
- Operating Frequency: 2.45GHz

---

## Objective
The Fabricated antenna was developed to validate the simulation and machine learning optimization results
through practical measurements using a Vector Network Analyzer (VNA)
